Magic Mirror Booth questions

Is the mirror booth good for a wedding?

It is the most-booked booth we have for weddings, largely because it works for every age in the room. Grandparents use it as happily as the dance floor crowd, and the full-length format means whole wedding parties can pile in rather than squeezing three people into a frame.

Can guests write on the mirror?

Yes. The touchscreen supports on-screen signatures and drawings, so guests can add a message to their print. It is a favorite at weddings where the couple keeps a copy of every photo in a guest book.

Can the magic mirror booth be set up outdoors?

Yes, when the space is level, dry and covered from direct weather. Because the mirror is a glass touchscreen, we avoid direct sun exposure — glare washes out the screen and makes the animations hard to see — so a shaded patio, tent or covered terrace works better than open sun. We'll walk through your outdoor space with you before booking to confirm it's a good fit.

How much does a photo booth rental cost in NYC?

Pricing depends on which booth you choose, how many hours you need, and where your venue is. Every quote is built for your specific event rather than pulled off a rate card, so the fastest way to a real number is the quote form — it takes about 30 seconds. Additional hours beyond your booked time are $125 per hour.
