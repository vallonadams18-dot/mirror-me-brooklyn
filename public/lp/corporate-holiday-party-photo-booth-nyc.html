Corporate holiday party photo booth NYC: your questions

How much does a corporate holiday party photo booth cost in NYC?

Pricing depends on the booth you choose, how long you need it, your date, branding, printing and other customization. Every quote is built for your specific event rather than pulled from a rate card, so the fastest way to a real number is the quote form. It takes about 30 seconds and there is no obligation.

Which photo booth is best for a company holiday party?

It depends on your venue, guest count and goals. The Pro / DSLR booth gives polished studio-quality portraits, the Magic Mirror is an interactive full-length experience for upscale rooms, Glam is elegant black-and-white, the AI booth creates custom scenes, 360 produces shareable video, Vogue is dramatic and fashion-inspired, and Roaming brings the camera to guests without a booth line. Tell us about your party and we will recommend an experience.

Can we add our company logo?

Yes. Custom digital and photo branding, such as your logo on photo overlays and print designs, is available. Start screens, branded galleries and other digital branding are available with applicable experiences. Physical booth branding may also be available depending on the experience you choose and is an additional service.

Can guests receive their photos instantly?

Yes. Guests can get their photos on their phones right away, typically by text, email or QR code, with an online gallery afterwards. We confirm the exact sharing options for the experience you pick.

Can you provide prints?

Yes. Printing is available with applicable experiences and packages. We confirm what is included for your selected experience in your quote.

Do you provide an attendant?

Yes. A trained attendant runs our booths for the full rental, setting up, helping guests and packing down, so nobody on your team has to look after it. We confirm staffing for your selected experience in your quote.

Can you provide a Certificate of Insurance?

Yes. When your venue requires a certificate of insurance, we can coordinate it. Send us the venue's contact details and requirements and we will handle the paperwork.

How much space does the photo booth require?

It varies by experience. Some booths need a dedicated footprint and a nearby power outlet, while the roaming photo experience needs no floor space at all. Once you have chosen an experience, we confirm the exact requirements against your venue.

How early should we book our holiday party photo booth?

As soon as your date and venue are set. November and December are popular for corporate events, and we confirm real availability when you request your date. If you want a physical booth wrap, allow two to three weeks for production; digital branding can usually be turned around faster.

Can you invoice our company or work to a PO?

Yes. We work with corporate clients regularly and can invoice your company. Account payment terms are available for established accounts; ask us for the details.
