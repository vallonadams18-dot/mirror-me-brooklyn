Vogue Booth questions

Does the Vogue booth print?

Yes. It is primarily a video booth, but guests can choose to print a still from their session, so you get both the shareable clip and something physical.

Can the LED lighting match our event colors?

It can. The interior lighting is programmable, so we can set it to your event palette or brand colors before doors open.

Can the vogue booth be used outdoors?

It can, though the LED interior lighting reads best in a dimmer environment — an evening outdoor event, a covered tent, or an indoor space works better than bright daylight. The structure also needs a dedicated 15 or 20 amp outlet and a level, dry surface, so tell us your outdoor setup in advance and we'll confirm the fit.

How much does a photo booth rental cost in NYC?

Pricing depends on which booth you choose, how many hours you need, and where your venue is. Every quote is built for your specific event rather than pulled off a rate card, so the fastest way to a real number is the quote form — it takes about 30 seconds.
