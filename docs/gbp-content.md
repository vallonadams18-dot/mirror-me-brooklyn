# Google Business Profile — copy-paste content

Drafted against Google's actual field limits: **business description 750
characters**, **service name 120**, **service description 300**. If you edit the
copy, keep it under the cap — the field silently refuses to save otherwise.

Nothing here repeats prose from the website verbatim. Reusable *structure* is
fine; reusable *prose* is what gets a profile treated as spun content.

## 3. Q&A seeds — 10

**Do this on a phone.** Q&A has no control in the GBP manager panel, and the
desktop Search knowledge panel does not show a Q&A section for this profile
(both verified 2026-08-24). Google surfaces it reliably only in the **Google
Maps app**: search the business, open the profile, scroll to **Questions &
answers**, tap **Ask a question**.

Post the question, then answer it from the business account so the reply carries
the "Owner" badge. Upvote each answer once — the most-upvoted answer is the one
Google displays. Asking from a second account (a colleague, a personal Gmail)
reads more naturally than asking and answering from the same one.

**Q: How much space do you need for a photo booth?**
> About 8 ft by 8 ft of clear space suits most of our experiences and gives guests room to pose comfortably. Working with less? Tell us the venue and we will recommend a setup that fits — the roaming booth needs no floor space at all.

**Q: What are the power requirements?**
> Most setups need one standard electrical outlet near the booth. Larger or specialty experiences can need more, and we confirm the exact requirement with you and your venue before the event.

**Q: How far in advance do you arrive to set up?**
> Our team arrives about an hour before your start time to set up and test everything. If you need us on site earlier for load-in or venue access, an idle fee of $150 per hour applies.

**Q: Do you serve areas outside Brooklyn?**
> Yes. We cover Brooklyn, Manhattan, Queens, the Bronx, Staten Island, Long Island, Westchester, New Jersey and Connecticut, and we travel beyond that for larger events. Travel fees depend on distance and load-in.

**Q: Are you insured, and can you provide a COI?**
> Yes. We carry a $3 million liability insurance policy. If your venue needs a certificate of insurance, or needs to be named as additional insured, send us the details before the event and we issue it the same day.

**Q: Do your rentals include an attendant?**
> Yes. Our staffed experiences include a trained attendant who handles setup, runs the booth, helps guests and breaks down at the end. For corporate events and activations they also coordinate with your planner or AV team.

**Q: Can guests get their photos on their phones?**
> Yes. Depending on the experience, guests receive photos, GIFs or videos instantly by text, email or QR code. Instant delivery needs internet at the venue; where it is unavailable we deliver afterwards.

**Q: Can the booth be branded for our company?**
> Yes, customization is one of our specialties. We wrap the booth in your colors and add your logo, campaign graphics, custom overlays, print designs and backdrops. Branded booths can also run surveys and opt-in data capture.

**Q: Can the photo booth be set up outdoors?**
> Yes, when weather and site conditions allow. We need a level, dry area with shelter from rain and direct sun, plus access to power. Tell us about the space and we will confirm what works.

**Q: How do we reserve a date?**
> Send us your event date, venue, event type and the experience you are interested in. We confirm availability and recommend options, and your date is reserved once the agreement and retainer are in place.

matches your Services against your category.

   `Photography service`). Highest-impact single edit on the profile.

   and the website both use (917) 724-6051.

   so there is a before-picture to measure against.
